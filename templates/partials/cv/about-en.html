I work at the intersection of climate policy, socio-ecological transformation and funding practice, with the goal of shaping effective and socially just transformation processes.

I deliberately move between politics, science, public administration and civil society. I use this work between systems to identify new thematic fields early, structure them strategically and translate them into impact-oriented programmes.

I feel most at home where new topics emerge, complex questions converge and there is still room to shape what comes next.
