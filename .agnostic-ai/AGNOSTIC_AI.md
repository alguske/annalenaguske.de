# annalenaguske.de

Personal site of Anna-Lena Guske, built with Zola. Content in `content/`, templates in `templates/`, styles in `sass/`, config in `config.toml`.

- Dev: `zola serve`. Build: `zola build` (output `public/`, ignored).
- Deploy: `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`.
- Commit rules: see the commit-conventions rule. Spec workflow: `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
